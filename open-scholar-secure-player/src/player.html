      About this player

      Open Scholar Secure Player
      Version 1.1 · Works offline after the first visit.

      What stays on this device

      Files you open are read on this device and never uploaded. Your library and saved places are kept only in this browser.

      Install the player

      Install it to get its own window and the cap icon. It opens without the browser bars and works without the internet.

      On iPhone: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

      How protection works

      Protected files show their limits before you view them. Once you start, these limits apply on purpose:

      • Copy, save, print and screenshot shortcuts are blocked.
      • Right-click, drag and text selection are turned off.
      • The view is hidden when you leave this window.
      • Your name and code appear as a watermark.
      • The file stops after its time or its number of openings.

      If the screen blurs or goes blank, that is the protection working. Click back into the player to continue.
