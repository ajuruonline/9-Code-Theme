# Open Scholar standard compliance

## Brand and interface

- Open Scholar deep-green palette and original cap.
- Dark headings on white or very light green.
- Mobile-first layout with no horizontal scrolling.
- SVG icons in the header and no decorative icon clutter.
- Square corners on bordered elements.
- Right-side advanced panels with a 10% top safe margin.
- `Powered by OpenScholar.ng.` footer.
- Extreme simplicity in front; complexity behind the interface.

## OS-STD-001

- Tutorial post created on first activation.
- Small, Medium and Big placement shortcodes.
- Public opening route and signed-in front-end owner route.
- Owner route requires `edit_posts`; Subscribers cannot create secure files.
- Source import, secure-file export, password-file export and recipient import.
- Settings and technical information collapsed by default.
- SVG administration and front-end controls.
- Tutorial and shortcodes available on first activation.

The plugin does not install a second phone app. `/secure-scholar/` opens through the existing Open Scholar site and shortcut. `/secure-scholar/owner/` is the signed-in online preparation route.
